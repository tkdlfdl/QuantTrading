# Recent Literature Sweep — 2024–2026 Only (compiled 2026-08)

**Purpose:** Registry has 115 entries; established anomalies are mined out. This sweep covers only the least-decayed layer: papers published/posted 2024–2026 (JF/JFE/RFS/JFQA/Management Science/Quantitative Finance/JPM/FAJ, arXiv q-fin, SSRN/NBER working papers), filtered against our data (US large-cap OHLCV daily 1997+/hourly 2019+; ^TNX/^FVX/^TYX/^IRX 1995+; 29-asset ETF panel incl. 8 country ETFs, GDX, XME, TIP, SHY; BTC/ETH 2014+; 11 sector SPDRs) and against the FULL-STACK bar (must be uncorrelated with: hourly contrarian MR core, daily+hourly momentum, cross-asset ETF momentum, overnight-share cross-section, 4-sigma event reversal, vol-target overlay + panic gate; must survive 0.1–0.25%/side).

**Method:** 6 parallel search angles (top journals, arXiv q-fin, ETF/cross-asset, rates×equity, short-horizon reversal, market structure), ~75 distinct web searches, every citation verified against primary sources (publisher page, arXiv, SSRN/NBER, author sites); an independent adversarial spot-check pass re-verified the five citations that anchor the Top-5 ranking. Working papers are marked WP (unrefereed); arXiv preprints are UNREPLICATED by default. **No reported number below was generated by us; per CLAUDE.md, nothing gets capital before an in-house backtest.**

## 2. TOP 5 — ranked by probability of clearing the full-stack bar

### #1 Rebalancing-flow front-run / turn-of-month overlay — P(clear) HIGH
- **Origin class:** flow/calendar timing (institutional 60/40 rebalancing).
- **Citations:** Harvey, Mazzoleni & Melone (NBER w33554, rev. Jan 2026); Kayacetin (JIFMIM 109, 2026). Both VERIFIED against primary text (NBER PDF read directly; JIFMIM abstract via Crossref).
- **Mechanism:** pensions/target-date funds rebalance on calendar+threshold rules; when equities are overweight vs 60/40 intra-month, forced equity selling depresses returns (17bps next day, per abstract); turn-of-month buying lifts TOM days (~10bps vs ~0; +7.8bps after high-vol periods, +20bps in recessions). Front-run strategy (long ES / short 10y futures on signals): 10.20%/yr excess, vol 9.17%, Sharpe 1.11 gross, "close to 1" net of costs, 0.90 ex-GFC/COVID (Table 6, 1997-09 to 2023-03).
- **Leak-free spec:** at close t compute month-to-date SPY total return minus synthetic 10y Treasury return (from Δ^TNX × duration ≈ 9, data 1995+; TIP/SHY as cross-check). If equity overweight beyond threshold in last ~3 sessions of month → reduce/short equity exposure into month-end, re-add over TOM window (last day + first 3 days). Signal known at close, trade next open/close. No lookahead: thresholds fixed ex-ante, calendar known.
- **Expected corr:** MR core ~0 (different horizon/universe); daily+hourly momentum ~0 (calendar-driven, sign flips); x-asset ETF momentum ~0–0.1 (both touch stock-bond relative returns — measure this one); overnight-share ~0; 4-sigma ~0; vol-target overlay small (both modulate net exposure — run as one combined exposure layer).
- **Implementation:** SPY/QQQ-level, ~24–50 trades/yr, cost-trivial at 0.1%/side. Backtest 1997–2026 daily. Risk: paper is futures-based WP; effect size modest — needs our own net numbers.

### #2 Pre-FOMC drift + FOMC-day rate-beta tilt — P(clear) HIGH (small size)
- **Origin class:** macro event calendar.
- **Citations:** Ignatieva & Ohashi (Applied Economics 57(17), 2025); Peng & Pan (SSRN 4764451); Dong, Han & Shi (SSRN 5762483); QuantSeeker OOS update through Dec 2024. Knox & Vissing-Jorgensen (FEDS 2026-23) as arbiter. All VERIFIED (WP magnitudes partly paywalled).
- **Mechanism:** uncertainty resolution before scheduled FOMC announcements → positive equity drift day before/of announcement (alive through 2024, concentrated in high-VIX periods); long-Treasury drift the day before; long-duration (high rate-beta) stocks earn extra announcement-day compensation.
- **Leak-free spec:** FOMC calendar is public ex-ante (8 meetings/yr). Base: hold SPY from close t-1 to announcement close. Cross-sectional add-on: rolling 250d beta of each large cap to Δ^TNX (estimable from our data); tilt long high-rate-beta names only over the announcement window. Optional gate: only when trailing VIX-proxy (realized vol) is elevated.
- **Expected corr:** ~0 to every book (invested ~5% of days; event-keyed). Tiny overlap with vol-target overlay if the panic gate happens to fire on FOMC days.
- **Implementation:** ~8–16 event-days/yr, cost-trivial. Standalone Sharpe only ~0.5–0.6 (practitioner OOS) — value is additive uncorrelated bps, not a headline book. Avoid the even-week FOMC-cycle variant (dead — Uppal 2025).

### #3 Price-path convexity cross-section — P(clear) MEDIUM
- **Origin class:** cross-sectional price-shape (overextrapolation).
- **Citation:** Gulen & Woeppel, JFQA 61(2), pp. 580–611, 2026 (online Jun 2025; erratum Oct 2025). VERIFIED, including magnitudes: the public SSRN version ("Price-Path Convexity, Extrapolation, and Short-Horizon Return Predictability," SSRN 3800419, early version w/ Zhi Da) reports a least-convex-minus-most-convex spread of **1.34–1.53% per month**, not driven by small stocks or bid-ask bounce.
- **Mechanism:** convexity (curvature) of the recent price path negatively predicts short-horizon returns at firm and market level, **independent of the cumulative return over the window**; attributed to investor overextrapolation of accelerating paths; per abstract not explained by microstructure/illiquidity → large-cap plausible.
- **Leak-free spec:** each day fit log-price on t and t² over trailing 20–60d per stock; signal = t² coefficient, cross-sectionally orthogonalized to the window's cumulative return (removes mechanical momentum overlap). Long bottom-decile convexity / short (or underweight) top decile; weekly–monthly rebalance; signal from data through close t-1, trade at t.
- **Expected corr:** MR core LOW-MEDIUM (both contrarian-flavored at short horizons — this is the kill risk; measure vs core daily P&L first); momentum books LOW (orthogonalized to cumulative return by construction); x-asset ~0; overnight-share ~0; 4-sigma LOW (both react to extreme paths — check overlap on event days); vol overlay ~0.
- **Implementation:** daily closes only, 1997–2026, our full 520-name panel. Monthly turnover survivable at 0.1–0.25%/side only if spread is decent — since magnitudes are unverified, this is a pure in-house empirical question. Also test the market-timing variant (index convexity) as a gate.

### #4 Crypto risk-appetite overlays (BTC/GLD ratio + weekend-crypto→Monday) — P(clear) MEDIUM-LOW
- **Origin class:** cross-asset lead-lag / risk-appetite regime (uses the NEW BTC/ETH + GLD data; distinct from the killed "crypto trend" — no crypto position is taken).
- **Citations:** Bouri & Demir (Finance Research Letters 81, 2025); Mourey, Shahrour & Șoiman (FRL 86, 2025). Both VERIFIED. No published strategy Sharpes — significance evidence only.
- **Mechanism:** BTC(or ETH)/gold ratio proxies aggregate risk appetite; its level/changes positively relate to subsequent US equity returns post-2020. Separately, weekend crypto returns/vol (crypto trades when equities don't) carry information for Monday equity opens; effect strengthened after mid-2022.
- **Leak-free spec:** (a) regime dial: z-score of log(BTC/GLD) over trailing 60–120d, computed Friday close, scales next-week net equity exposure; (b) Monday overlay: Sat–Sun BTC/ETH return + realized vol computed before Monday open, scales Monday exposure (never a standalone round trip — overlay on positions we already hold, so marginal cost ≈ 0).
- **Expected corr:** ~0 to all books (conditioning variable is a market our books don't touch); interacts with vol-target overlay (both are exposure dials) — implement inside the same exposure layer and measure incremental value there.
- **Weaknesses:** FRL-tier venues; post-2020 regime only (fails our earliest-data rule for long history — backtest 2014+ and report the pre/post-2020 split honestly); mechanism could die if crypto-equity correlation regime shifts.

### #5 End-of-day reversal — as CORE ENTRY-TIMING UPGRADE, not a new sleeve — P(add value) HIGH, P(standalone) ~0
- **Origin class:** intraday cross-sectional reversal (retail attention + short-cover into close).
- **Citations:** Baltussen, Da & Soebhag (SSRN 5039009, 2024–25, Quantpedia Award). Primary PDF states L/S returns of **3.78–6.86 bps/day (9.5–17.3%/yr) gross**, VW vs EW, Jan 1993–Dec 2019, "primarily positive price pressure on intraday losers" (winner decile ≈ 0). A circulating "~24bps/day" figure was checked against the PDF and REFUTED. Execution support: Goyal, Jegadeesh & Wu (JFQA 2026: closing-auction impact 9–21bps annualized ex-microcaps, cheapest venue). Both VERIFIED.
- **Honest classification:** correlated with the MR core BY DESIGN → fails the new-sleeve bar. Ranked here because it is the highest-probability *upgrade* to the 75%-capital core: two legs in 30 minutes at 0.1–0.25%/side kills it standalone, but our core already pays entry costs — shifting WHEN it enters is free.
- **Leak-free spec:** compute the core's oversold ranking on the 15:00 hourly bar; execute entries at/near the closing auction (MOC) instead of uniformly intraday, capturing the documented 15:30–16:00 loser rebound and the cheapest venue. No extra data, no extra trades.
- **Expected effect:** improves core fill quality + captures a documented last-half-hour tailwind on exactly the names we buy. Measure as core-variant A/B over 2019–2026 hourly data.

## 3. Core defense package (Book: hourly contrarian MR, 75% of capital)

2024–2026 evidence says RAW short-term reversal has decayed but properly-measured reversal has not — and passive growth structurally strengthens it (Höfler-Schlag-Schmeling). Five OHLCV-only upgrades, in evidence order:

1. **Industry-adjust / residualize the score** before ranking (Stosik & Zaremba 2026: industry-adjusted 0.53%/mo, Sharpe 0.74 while raw ≈ +0.05%/mo across 64 countries — abstract-verified; their cost-survival claim not yet verified against full text; Brogaard-Han-Kim residual reversal; Farkas-Neszveda industry-laggard 2x). Use sector-SPDR (or peer-mean) demeaning of hourly returns.
2. **Gap vs intraday decomposition of the crash** (Jegadeesh-Luo-Subrahmanyam-Titman RFS 2025; Barardehi et al.): skip or downweight extremes driven by overnight gaps (news proxy); fade intraday-driven moves (noise).
3. **Vol/turnover-conditioned holds** (Dai-Medhat-Novy-Marx-Rizova FAJ 2024): high-realized-vol names → keep short 8h-style holds; low-turnover names → longer holds or skip.
4. **MAX filter** (Chen et al. JEF 2025): prefer fading names with an extreme single-day move in the lookback (reversal ~2.5x stronger).
5. **Screens** (Chen-Stivers-Sun JEF 2024): avoid fading high-turnover names near 52-week highs.

Each is a parameter-light variant testable on existing 2019–2026 hourly infra; per CLAUDE.md report old-vs-new side by side.
